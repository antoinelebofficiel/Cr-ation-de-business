10-pack of natural gray cellulose kitchen sponges.

They ship compressed — thin as cards. Add water and they puff up in a few seconds into a full-size sponge. Easy to store, no bulky pack sitting under the sink.

Wood-pulp cellulose: good foam, soaks up water and grease, won’t scratch non-stick pans, dishes, glass, sinks, or tubs. Kitchen, bathroom, car — same sponge.

After you use one, rinse it, squeeze it lengthwise (don’t wring it or it can tear), and let it dry. Less funk than a regular synthetic sponge.
