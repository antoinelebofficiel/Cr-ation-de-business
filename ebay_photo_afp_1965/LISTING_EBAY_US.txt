Original 1965 AFP press photo of President Lyndon B. Johnson handing the pen to Martin Luther King Jr. after the Voting Rights Act signing in Washington.

This is a period wire photograph from Agence France-Presse, not a later reprint. Front shows the close-up of the pen handoff; King is smiling, LBJ’s arm coming in from the left, other officials behind them.

On the back you’ll find the French AFP caption dated 7/8/1965 (August 7, 1965):

“WASHINGTON — Le Président LYNDON B. JOHNSON remet le stylo à MARTIN LUTHER KING après avoir signé la loi sur le droit de vote des Noirs.”

Also on the verso: circular AGENCE FRANCE-PRESSE / SERVICE PHOTOGRAPHIQUE stamp (24 rue Vivienne, Paris), AFP copyright wording, “Papier Mimosa,” handwritten ref. 78.431, and a small ink annotation. That’s the usual newsroom backside on these.

Condition is what you’d expect from a working press print from the ’60s — soft corners, light handling marks, some age spotting on the reverse. Nothing crazy, but it isn’t museum-mint. All of that is visible in the photos.
